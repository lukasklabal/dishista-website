Frequently asked questions

How much does Dishista cost?

Dishista costs $3.99 as a one-time purchase. You pay once when you download it from the App Store or Google Play — there's no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Dishista works without signing up. Your profile, favourites and meal plans are saved on your device.

Does Dishista work offline?

Yes. All recipes and photos are part of the app, so you can browse and cook without an internet connection.

How do I change the language?

Dishista starts in your phone's language if it's one of the ten we support. To switch, go to Profile (the avatar at the top right) → Language or Settings → Language. The whole app and every recipe switch over. Arabic is shown right to left — after switching, just restart the app once.

How does the meal-plan guide work?

Open the Plan tab and start a conversation. The guide asks about your goal, how long the plan should last, which meals to plan, your calorie target, diet, ingredients you avoid, cooking time and favourite cuisines. It then builds a plan from our recipes and explains its choices. Everything runs on your device.

You can swap or remove any meal, add a recipe from its page with Add to meal plan, or start from one of the ready-made themed plans.

How does the shopping list work?

In a recipe, tap Add to shopping list and pick the ingredients — amounts follow your servings. From a meal plan you can shop for today, tomorrow, the week or the whole plan. Matching ingredients are added up and the list is sorted by supermarket aisle.

For ingredients from world cuisines we tell you where to find them in your country. You can also share the list as text.

Can I save my own recipes?

Yes. In Profile → Your own recipes you can add a recipe with a photo, ingredients, method and tips. Just paste the ingredients as text, timers are set from the method and we suggest allergens from the ingredients. Your recipes work everywhere — in cooking mode, in meal plans and in the shopping list.

How does allergy mode work?

In Settings → Allergies & intolerances, pick what you can't eat — any of the 14 EU allergens or lactose. Recipes that contain them are then hidden or clearly flagged in lists, search and meal plans. Each recipe shows what it contains and what to check on the label. The information is a guide — with a serious allergy, always read the product labels.

Is the meal plan medical or nutritional advice?

No. Plans are general inspiration based on recipe data, and calorie values are approximate per serving. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or follow a special diet, please talk to a doctor or registered dietitian.

Can I hide recipes I can't or don't want to eat?

Yes. In Settings → Diet & tastes choose your diet, the ingredients you avoid and how spicy you like it. You can show a warning on those recipes or hide them completely.

Where do the photos come from?

Recipe photos come from Wikimedia Commons and are used under free licences (CC BY, CC BY-SA or public domain). The author and licence are shown under every photo, and you'll find the full list in Settings → About → Photo credits. If you are the author of a photo and would like it credited differently or removed, write to us at dishistaapp@gmail.com.

Deleting your data

Dishista doesn't have accounts and stores your data only on your device, so you can delete it yourself at any time:

  • Open Settings → Privacy & data and tap Delete all data. Your profile, saved recipes, cooked dishes, meal plans and settings are removed immediately.
  • Or simply uninstall the app — all of its data is removed with it.

We don't keep a copy anywhere. If you have emailed us, ask and we'll delete that conversation too. See our privacy policy for details.
