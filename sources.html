Transparency

Our sources

Dishista is built on trustworthy, publicly available sources. Here is where our recipes, photos, maps and allergen information come from.

Recipes

Every recipe was written by the Dishista team, following the traditional methods of each cuisine and adapted to ingredients you can actually buy. Calorie values per serving are estimates.

Photos

Dish photos come from Wikimedia Commons and are used under free licences (CC BY, CC BY-SA and public domain). The author and licence of every photo are listed in the app and in the photo credits at the bottom of this page.

Allergens and intolerances

Each recipe lists the 14 major allergens defined by the EU plus lactose. The information is based on the recipe's ingredients and is indicative — always read the labels of shop-bought products. Our mini encyclopedia of allergens and intolerances draws on these public sources:
